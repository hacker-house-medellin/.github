# Voluntary resident workshift rent discounts

Status: proposed operating policy; management and local legal review are required before use.

Hacker House Medellín may offer a clearly defined rent discount to a resident who voluntarily commits to a recurring house workshift. Suitable work may include cooking shared meals, cleaning common areas, tidying, organizing, or decorating. A discount is earned only under a written agreement; informal favors and ordinary expectations for keeping one's own room or cleaning up after oneself are not workshifts.

## Fair access and evidence of skill

- Management publishes the available role, expected standard, schedule, approximate time, discount, trial period, and selection criteria before choosing a resident.
- Applicants should provide at least two relevant references when reasonably available, or equivalent evidence such as prior paid or volunteer experience, a portfolio, certifications, or a supervised practical demonstration. References are used only to assess the workshift and are not a condition of tenancy.
- Management obtains consent before contacting a reference, collects only the minimum information needed, and never places reference contact details or reports in a public repository, public issue, or general project note.
- Selection and evaluation must be consistent, documented, accessible, and unrelated to protected characteristics. Reasonable accommodations and non-reference evidence must be available so that the process does not unfairly exclude newer residents or people without a conventional work history.

## Written workshift agreement

Before work begins, management and the resident sign a separate, plain-language agreement that states:

1. the exact tasks, shared spaces, quality checklist, food-safety or product-safety rules, and work that is explicitly out of scope;
2. the schedule, expected hours, handoff procedure, approved substitutes, and how an illness, emergency, house closure, or missed shift is handled;
3. the fixed rent discount or credit, when it appears on the resident's account, any lawful tax treatment, and how partial periods are calculated;
4. the start date, bounded trial period, review dates, end date, renewal process, and notice that either side must give to end the arrangement;
5. the supplies, protective equipment, training, accessibility accommodations, and reimbursement that management provides; and
6. a private feedback, dispute, incident, and appeal path with a named management contact.

The resident receives a copy before accepting. Terms may not be changed retroactively. Management records completion with a minimal checklist or ordinary human review; cameras, microphones, facial recognition, location history, or covert monitoring must never be used to grade a resident's workshift.

## Safety, tenancy, and employment boundary

- Participation is voluntary. Declining, ending, or being ineligible for a workshift must not threaten housing, essential services, community access, immigration support, deposits, or an existing lease.
- A missed or disputed shift does not create a new debt, penalty, or surprise rent increase. Any prospective change to a discount follows the written agreement, applicable lease, notice requirements, and a documented appeal process.
- Residents do not handle hazardous repairs, security enforcement, controlled substances, private resident records, or other regulated work unless separately qualified, insured, trained, and legally authorized.
- Cooking roles require an appropriate food-safety plan, allergy and dietary controls, clean facilities, and management-provided supplies. Cleaning roles require safe products, labels, ventilation, protective equipment, and training.
- A rent credit must not be used to evade wage, employment, contractor, tax, insurance, housing, health, or occupational-safety law. If the actual relationship is employment, management must use the required employment agreement, pay, benefits, protections, and records in addition to or instead of a rent discount.

## Records and review

Management keeps the minimum private record needed for the agreement, completed shifts, credits, safety training, and disputes. Access is role-limited, retention is time-bounded, and the resident can request a copy or correction. Public planning tools contain only policy and delivery status, never applicant names, reference details, schedules that reveal occupancy, performance reports, or housing records.

Management reviews this policy at least annually and after any safety, fairness, privacy, or legal incident. No program should launch until local housing and labor counsel has confirmed the agreement and accounting model for Medellín, Colombia.
